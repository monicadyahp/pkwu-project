About Our Project

Welcome to our Entrepreneurship Showcase!

We are high school students learning the fundamentals of business, product design, and digital marketing. This website is built as part of our final project for the Entrepreneurship (PKWU) subject.

Our mission is to simulate a real-world e-commerce experience by creating, marketing, and selling our handcrafted products. By supporting us, you are contributing to our learning journey in becoming future young entrepreneurs!
